About

About

Researcher in X‑ray metrology and semiconductor process control, working across synchrotron facilities and in‑house compact X‑ray sources. The in‑house platform includes vacuum system development for soft and tender X‑ray instrumentation. Applied techniques span SAXS, GISAXS, XRR, and HRXRD, targeting advanced logic device characterization at sub‑5 nm nodes.

My current work focuses on building and advancing X-ray metrology capabilities for critical dimension analysis and structural characterization of Gate‑All‑Around (GAA) nanosheet architectures. This involves reciprocal space modeling, scattering physics, and custom Python‑based analysis pipelines to translate diffraction signatures into actionable process insights, complemented by SEM and TEM for ground‑truth validation of non‑destructive X‑ray measurements.

My broader engineering foundation covers precision metrology systems and machine design, supported by numerical simulation (ANSYS, FDTD/Lumerical), bridging fundamental physics with engineering‑scale metrology challenges.
