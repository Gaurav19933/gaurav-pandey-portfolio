About

About

I am a curious researcher with a strong passion for semiconductor industry research as well as other high-tech evolving industries. My interests focus on designing and building automated optical inspection and mechanical systems, with particular attention to metrology and inspection tools for semiconductor applications.

I enjoy the mathematical modeling and synthesis of physics behind these systems. I actively look for important insights and signature patterns shaped by quantum and classical light-matter interactions. Python, MATLAB, and C are my preferred languages for algorithm development and data analysis.

For system design, I work comfortably with computer-aided design software such as SolidWorks and NX Unigraphics. I also use numerical and GUI-based engineering tools such as ANSYS Workbench, FDTD Lumerical methods, and FDM / FEM workflows for analysis, optimization, and engineering development.

Currently, I am specializing in X-ray metrology and CD metrology with modeling and simulation analysis. Through these projects, I aim to contribute to advances in metrology and drive innovation in the semiconductor industry.
